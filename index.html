03For families

Getting care started, step by step

Most families call us in the middle of a hard week. Here's exactly what happens next.

01First visit

Free consultation

We sit down with you and your loved one to understand the routine, the health picture, and what would make daily life easier.

02The plan

A tailored care plan

We build a plan for one person — hours, tasks, and level of care — and match it to the Angel whose skills and temperament fit best.

03Ongoing care

Care at home, adjusted as needed

Your daily needs become our responsibility. As things change, the plan changes with them — you stay in the loop throughout.

04Everything we can help with

Anne Arundel County and surrounding areas

A reference list of the tasks our Angels take on — not a menu you have to choose from. Need something that isn't here? Ask, and we'll accommodate it.

Company & wellbeing

  • Companionship and conversation
  • Respite care
  • Monitoring nutrition
  • Morning wake-up and evening tuck-in
  • Stimulate mental awareness
  • Assist with clothing selection
  • Appointment reminders and scheduling
  • Accompany to the doctor and document advice
  • Crafts, games, and activities
  • Alzheimer’s care
  • Assist with walking

Home & meals

  • Plan, prepare, and clean up meals
  • Prepare grocery lists and meal planning
  • Record and arrange recipes
  • Check food expiration dates
  • Laundry and ironing
  • Change linens and make beds
  • Dust furniture and organize closets
  • Take out garbage
  • Care for houseplants
  • Supervise home maintenance
  • Assist with pet care

Errands, mail & outings

  • Escort for shopping and errands
  • Pick up prescriptions
  • Drop off and pick up dry cleaning
  • Mail bills and letters
  • Write letters and correspondence
  • Organize mail
  • Maintain the calendar
  • Plan visits, outings, and trips
  • Visits to neighbors and friends
  • Escort to services, plays, and concerts
  • Medication reminders and administration*

*Medication is administered only by caregivers qualified to do so, always under the supervision of a registered nurse.
